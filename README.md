## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

### 1. Is the MindValley free trial really free?

Yes, the 15-day trial is completely free. You won’t be charged if you cancel before the trial ends. Just make sure to cancel in time if you don’t want to continue.

### 2. Do I need to enter payment details for the free trial?

Yes, you’ll need to provide payment information to start the trial. However, no charges will be made until the 15-day trial period is over.

### 3. Can I access all courses during the free trial?

Yes, the All Access Membership trial gives you full access to over 100 courses and 1,000+ meditations, except for a few partner programs.

### 4. How do I cancel my MindValley trial?

To cancel, go to your account settings on the MindValley website and follow the cancellation instructions. Do this before the 15-day trial ends to avoid charges.

### 5. What happens after the free trial ends?

If you don’t cancel, you’ll be charged for the membership plan you selected (monthly or yearly). You’re still covered by a 15-day money-back guarantee if you change your mind.
